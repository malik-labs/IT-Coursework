# Unit 12: IT Technical Support and Management

**Grade:** Distinction &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Assignments:** 2

**The brief:**

- **Assignment 1:** write a report for my manager evaluating the support and management needs of the IT systems in two organisations: one that handles support in-house and one that outsources it, each with different users and systems.
- **Assignment 2:** explain and carry out everyday maintenance tasks that keep a PC fast, secure and reliable, and evaluate their effect.

## Assignment 1: Support needs of two organisations

A report of about 12,000 words. It covers:

| Area | What I covered |
|---|---|
| **IT systems and support** | The purpose of IT support, and how it's both **reactive** (fixing faults raised through tickets) and **proactive** (monitoring and security updates). Challenges such as peak-time ticket volumes and poor ticket routing. |
| **Different users** | How support differs for beginners, expert users and administrators. |
| **Continuity** | Internal, external and cloud storage, with the pros and cons of each. |
| **Networks** | LAN, WAN and PAN, virtual PCs, and email communications. |
| **Support lifecycle** | SaaS, keeping software current, licensing, design and testing, operation and decommissioning, resources, rising demand and user support. |
| **Safe working practices** | Testing equipment, PAT testing, display screen equipment (DSE) rules, manual handling, electrical and static risks, fire, and workstation ergonomics. |
| **Job roles** | What **1st, 2nd and 3rd-line** support each handle, and how issues escalate between them. |
| **Support tools** | Monitoring tools such as Nagios, SolarWinds and PRTG, desktop management, disk image deployment, asset management and remote desktop access. |

I finished with an evaluation of what happens when fewer technicians are available, how networks and servers depend on each other, and why computers, networks and offline backups matter to staff.

## Assignment 2: Everyday PC maintenance

A guide to 12 maintenance and support tasks. For each one I explained what it does and answered questions about its effect, such as whether files opened faster or how long the PC took to start:

- Disk defragmentation
- RAM
- Disk Cleanup
- Antivirus scans
- Limiting startup apps
- Clearing cookies
- Upgrading an HDD to an SSD
- Security settings
- Windows updates
- Connecting to Wi-Fi
- Archiving
- Control Panel

I also covered time management for support staff.

### Issue log

For four of the tasks I logged the fault like a helpdesk ticket, with a description, category, priority, the dates it was opened and closed, and the fix:

| # | Issue | Category | Priority | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Files take a long time to open | Both | Medium | Ran Disk Defragmenter |
| 2 | PC is slow and out of storage | Both | Medium | Ran Disk Cleanup |
| 3 | PC is slow, with constant pop-up adverts | Both | Medium | Ran a full antivirus scan to find and remove threats |
| 4 | Lots of apps open at startup, slowing the PC | Software | Medium | Disabled unused startup apps in Settings |

I've since recorded several of these tasks as video guides: see [Windows Support How-Tos](https://github.com/malik-labs/Windows-Support-How-Tos).

## What I learned

- **How support is tiered.** 1st line resolves common issues quickly and escalates what needs deeper investigation to 2nd and 3rd line.
- **Support isn't only fixing faults.** Monitoring, updates and backups stop many issues from happening.
- **Safety is part of the job.** PAT testing, DSE rules, manual handling and static precautions all apply to IT technicians.
- **Match the fix to the hardware.** Defragmenting helps hard drives (HDDs) but isn't useful for SSDs.
